Hi, I'm Shubham Jadhav.

I build interfaces for a living — from marketing sites that need to convert, to dashboards that need to stay legible under real data. This page is the longer version of my story.

01 — Profile

A bit about me

I'm a frontend developer who cares about the small details: the way a button responds to a hover, the rhythm of spacing down a page, the difference between a site that merely works and one that feels considered. I spend most of my time in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, turning designs and rough ideas into interfaces that hold up on any screen size.

I got into this field because I liked that you could build something on a Friday night and have someone, anywhere, open it in a browser by Saturday morning. That immediacy hasn't worn off. These days I split my time between freelance client work and personal projects that let me try out new layout techniques and interaction patterns before I bring them to production work.

02 — Approach

How I work

A few principles that guide every project, regardless of size.

01

Content first

Layout decisions follow from what actually needs to be said and shown — never the other way around.

02

Built to last

Semantic markup and clean CSS mean the site is still easy to extend a year from launch, not just at launch.

03

Fast by default

Lightweight pages, minimal dependencies, and careful asset choices keep load times honest.
